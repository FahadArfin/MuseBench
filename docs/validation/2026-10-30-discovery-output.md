# CS02 useful discovery output validation — 30 October 2026

This source change preserves the existing native Muse backend and original discovery result/schema validation. It adds text-only lossy previews after validation, with source/tool/catalog identity and completed/failed outcome, a 16,000-code-point ceiling including the omission marker, explicit omitted-code-point/non-text-block counts, and the original fully encoded frame size in bytes. The existing conservative 128-character request-ID reserve now includes the emitted JSONL newline. Fully encoded responses remain at most 131,072 bytes; transport receive budgets were not expanded.

Small results retain the complete original wire shape. Oversized output omits media, metadata and structured content, never publishes partial `structuredContent`, and explains when no eligible text exists. A source-provided `truncated:true` is separately attributed in the notice. Invalid original structured content remains unknown, closes the source and is not retried.
